# Growth Audit — Grammarly

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/grammarly-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Grammarly already has, plus a clickable prototype of the iPhone onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Grammarly's own design. Prepared by Adapty, September 2026.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask for the account after the purchase | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Put what they write in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Show your 4.7 and Apple's badges on the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications once the keyboard works | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Offer three months when they close | ARPU +10–15% |

Every screen in the prototype keeps Grammarly's art direction. Only the order changed, plus a few things Grammarly already owns but does not show at the right moment.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, award and feature line comes from Grammarly's own App Store listing, website, plans page and in-app screens.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Grammarly.
